# Monetization

The project should be useful without feeling predatory.

People are worried about losing something personal. Pricing should feel fair,
simple, and calm.

## Principles

- Keep a useful free version.
- Do not charge people to export their own data.
- Avoid fear-based pricing.
- Make paid features feel like convenience, not ransom.
- Be clear about what restoration can and cannot do.

## Option 1: Free Basic App

The free version can include:

- Manual backups.
- Local storage.
- JSON export.
- Basic playlist backup.
- Basic restore planning.

This helps build trust and proves the product is useful.

## Option 2: One-Time Pro Upgrade

A simple paid upgrade could unlock:

- Unlimited snapshots.
- Better change history.
- Advanced matching tools.
- CSV export.
- M3U-style playlist export.
- Batch playlist rebuild.

Suggested price: $9 to $19 one time.

This is easy to understand and avoids subscription fatigue.

## Option 3: Low-Cost Subscription

A small subscription could cover features with ongoing costs:

- Cloud backup.
- Automatic scheduled backups.
- Cross-device sync.
- Restore reminders.
- Priority matching improvements.

Suggested price: $1 to $3 per month, or $10 to $24 per year.

This should only exist if there are real ongoing costs.

## Option 4: Tip Jar

Let users support the project voluntarily.

This works well if the app starts as a small indie tool or open-source project.

Suggested options:

- $3 thanks.
- $8 supporter.
- $15 super supporter.

## Option 5: Family Upgrade

Some households share music habits across multiple Apple accounts.

A family upgrade could allow multiple local profiles on the same device or
shared CloudKit backups.

Suggested price: $19 to $29 one time, or a small yearly plan.

## What Not To Do

- Do not block exports behind a paywall.
- Do not charge a high monthly fee for basic backup.
- Do not imply that paying guarantees perfect restore.
- Do not store user library data on our servers unless the user clearly opts in.
- Do not sell listening or library data.

## Best Starting Model

Start with a free app plus a one-time Pro upgrade.

Add a small subscription only if cloud backup and scheduled backups create real
ongoing costs.
