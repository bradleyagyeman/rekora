# Naming

The working name for this project is Rekora.

## Why Rekora

Rekora is short, clear, and easy to say.

It hints at "record" without sounding like a database tool. That fits the heart
of the project: helping people keep a record of the music, albums, and
playlists they care about.

It is also more unique than a plain English word, which should help with app
name, domain, and search availability.

It also works in simple product language:

- Rekora snapshot.
- Restore from Rekora.
- Export with Rekora.
- Changes in Rekora.

## Naming Council Summary

Three rounds of naming discussion compared warm, safe, music-related, and made
up names.

The strongest candidates from the first naming round were:

- Trove.
- Refrain.
- Kura.
- Keepsake.
- Harbor.

Trove originally won because it was clear without being scary or technical.
After review, it felt too likely to be taken as a domain or crowded in search.

Rekora replaced it because it keeps the idea of preserving a record, but feels
more ownable.

## Runner-Up Names

Refrain was the best music-related option. The concern is that it can also mean
to stop or avoid doing something, which is not ideal for a music backup app.

Kura was the most distinctive short option. The concern is that most users
would not know the meaning without explanation.

Keepsake was warm, but it felt more like a photo or memory app.

Harbor felt safe, but too broad.

Trove was clear, but too generic for a public product name.

## Important Check

Before using Rekora publicly, we should check:

- App Store name availability.
- Domain availability.
- Trademark risk.
- Similar music or backup products using the same name.
